About Riverbend

Meet the people and thoughtful choices that make a first visit feel easier.

Practice for everyday people

Riverbend is a neighborhood studio built around a simple idea: a yoga practice should leave room for different bodies, schedules, and levels of experience.

Our approach is calm and collaborative. You choose how much to participate, and instructors offer options rather than a single version of a pose that everyone must perform.

Meet the instructor team

Maya Chen: gentle and restorative practice

Maya welcomes first-time students with clear instructions, unhurried transitions, and time for questions. She offers chair and prop options so students can explore a comfortable starting point.

Jordan Reed: flow and private sessions

Jordan creates practical sequences for busy days. His classes include choices to adjust the pace and space for students returning after a break from practice.

Access and accommodations

  • Chairs, blocks, and cushions can support different positions.
  • Seated alternatives and shorter sequences are welcome.
  • Instructors can explain a movement before students try it.
  • Hands-on guidance is offered only with the student's agreement.

Ask about entry, seating, sound, or other access needs before visiting. We can discuss the space and suitable session options.

Discuss your first visit

Studio policies

  • Arrive ten minutes early for a first visit and introduction.
  • Wear clothing that allows you to move comfortably.
  • Silence phones and respect other students' personal space.
  • Rest, change a movement, or leave a sequence whenever needed.
  • Please request booking changes a day before a session.
