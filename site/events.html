Monthly workshops

These sample recurring events offer time to learn and ask questions.

Your First Yoga Visit

First Saturday of each month, 10:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m..

Explore the studio, meet an instructor, and try simple seated and standing movements. No previous yoga experience is needed.

Desk-to-Mat Reset

Second Wednesday of each month, 6:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m..

A practical introduction to an unhurried practice after a busy day, with chair options and time to explore your own pace.

Rest and Restore

Last Sunday of each month, 3:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m..

Learn how cushions and blocks can support a quieter practice.
